PROMPT 1

IF U HAVE AN IDEA .....copy this and paste with ur idea ⏬

You are my creative co-founder for a startup pitch competition called "Fund My Crazy," where students reimagine a piece of city infrastructure and pitch it as a fundable idea. Here is the THEME: [type your theme here — e.g. Roads & Transportation] ROUGH IDEA: [type your idea here in 2 lines] Your job: take my raw idea and turn it into a genuinely winning entry. Judges score on: - Vision (30%) — an extraordinary conceptual leap, not an incremental fix - Real-life Relevance (20%) — solves an actual, specific pain people feel - Future-Focused (15%) — solves tomorrow's problem today, not yesterday's - Execution (15%) — a real, doable plan, not just a daydream Do the following, in this exact structure: 1. WHY THIS ELEMENT, WHY NOW (form-ready answer, ~60-80 words) 1st point - A single bold, quotable sentence that sells the idea in a room in 3 seconds. 2nd point - Write this as a direct answer I can paste into the submission form field "Why this element, why now?" 2. WHAT DOES YOUR REIMAGINED CITY LOOK LIKE (form-ready answer, ~80-100 words) Paint the picture — how it works, what it feels like to use, described vividly enough that someone can visualize it without seeing the image yet. 3. HOW GEMINI IS INTEGRATED (form-ready answer, ~60-80 words) Explain plausibly how Gemini would be used in the real system (e.g. reading patterns, understanding language/voice, predicting behavior, personalizing something) — specific enough to sound real, not generic AI buzzwords. 4. WHY THIS MATTERS FOR THE FUTURE (form-ready answer, ~60-80 words) Explain who it helps, how it fits into cities, and what makes it a believable reality rather than just a cool concept. 5. NANO BANANA IMAGE PROMPT Write one ready-to-paste image generation prompt, following this exact formula: - title on the image(looks good) - Name the exact element being reimagined - Specify its function, vibe, and energy (how it should feel to use) - Say who it's for and the emotion they feel using it - End with the 1-2 line bold claim from step 8 Write it as flowing descriptive prose (not a bullet list) suitable for direct image generation, emphasizing visual details: setting, lighting, people, technology visible, mood. Keep everything sharp, specific, and free of generic AI/startup clichés like "revolutionize," "seamless," "cutting-edge." Sound like a smart, slightly obsessive student who has actually thought this through — not a corporate brochure.

PROMPT 2

IF U DON'T HAVE ANY IDEA ... COPY PASTE THIS ⏬

AND JST SELECT 1 OR 2 OR 3

You are my creative co-founder for a startup pitch competition called "Fund My Crazy," where students reimagine a piece of city infrastructure or system and pitch it as a fundable idea. I don't have an idea yet. We'll do this in two stages — do NOT skip ahead to Stage 2 until I tell you which idea I'm picking. CITY: Chennai, India THEMES TO PULL FROM: Roads & Transportation, Community & Living, Healthcare, Education, Markets (or "Surprise Us" if something genuinely wild fits none of these) Judges score on: - Vision (30%) — an extraordinary conceptual leap, not an incremental fix - Real-life Relevance (20%) — solves an actual, specific pain people feel - Future-Focused (15%) — solves tomorrow's problem today, not yesterday's - Execution (15%) — a real, doable plan, not just a daydream ============================================================ STAGE 1 — GENERATE 3 IDEAS ============================================================ Give me 3 distinct ideas, each rooted in a real, specific problem Chennai residents actually deal with (traffic, flooding, waste, heat, water scarcity, healthcare access, informal markets, power cuts, etc. — pick whichever gives the sharpest idea per concept, don't force every idea into the same bucket). For each idea give me: 1. A one-line hook with its theme 2. The specific pain point it kills — name the street, season, neighborhood, or group of people. No vague generalities. 3. The conceptual leap — what makes this a genuine reimagining, not a patch on the current system 4. A 3-4 sentence gut-check on feasibility — could this actually be piloted, and roughly how Rank the 3 by how likely they are to win, and say why the top one wins. Then STOP and ask me: "Which one — 1, 2, or 3?" ============================================================ STAGE 2 — FULL SUBMISSION (only after I reply with 1, 2, or 3) ============================================================ Once I pick, take that idea and build the complete, form-ready submission in this exact structure: 1. WHY THIS ELEMENT, WHY NOW (~60-80 words) Open with a single bold, quotable sentence that sells the idea in a room in 3 seconds. Then write the rest as a direct answer I can paste into the submission field "Why this element, why now?" 2. WHAT DOES YOUR REIMAGINED CITY LOOK LIKE (~80-100 words) Paint the picture — how it works, what it feels like to use, vivid enough that someone can visualize it without seeing an image yet. 3. HOW GEMINI IS INTEGRATED (~60-80 words) Explain plausibly how Gemini would power the real system — e.g. reading patterns, understanding voice/language, predicting behavior, personalizing something. Specific enough to sound real, not generic AI buzzwords. 4. WHY THIS MATTERS FOR THE FUTURE (~60-80 words) Who it helps, how it fits into cities, what makes it a believable reality rather than just a cool concept. 5. NANO BANANA IMAGE PROMPT One ready-to-paste image generation prompt, written as flowing descriptive prose (not bullets), covering: - A title to render on the image - The exact element being reimagined - Its function, vibe, and energy — how it feels to use - Who it's for and the emotion they feel using it - Ending with the bold one-line claim from section 1 Emphasize setting, lighting, people, visible technology, mood. ============================================================ TONE RULES (apply to everything, both stages) ============================================================ - No generic AI/startup clichés: "revolutionize," "seamless," "cutting-edge," "empower," "game-changing," "disrupt" - Sound like a smart, slightly obsessive student who has actually walked these streets and thought this through — not a corporate brochure - Be specific over impressive. A concrete detail beats a grand claim - Push back and tighten anything vague rather than padding it
